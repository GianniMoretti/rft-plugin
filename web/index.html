No snippets or documents yet. They turn verified notes into writing, at two levels. Snippets are the building blocks: atomic LaTeX resting on the notes they state, written once and reused, so a definition reads the same everywhere. Documents (a paper, a thesis chapter) are built with them: each holds copies of snippets, adapted to its narrative as you like, and text of its own; a copy whose snippet changes is flagged for review, never rewritten.

Both are yours to write: Claude proposes snippets from verified notes and where to place them, comments and suggests, and writes only where you unlock it.
